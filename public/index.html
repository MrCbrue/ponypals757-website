How a party works

Most families book two to four weeks ahead. Weekends in May and June go first, so earlier is better if you have a date in mind.

  1. Send us the details

    Use the form below. Tell us the date, where the party is, and anything about the birthday kid we should know. You don't pay anything yet.

  2. We call you back within a day

    We'll confirm the date, ask about your yard and gate, and answer questions. A $50 deposit holds the slot and is refundable up to a week before.

  3. We arrive 20 minutes early

    The ponies show up groomed and saddled. One of us holds the lead on every ride, and every rider wears a helmet.

  4. We clean up and head home

    We put down mats where the ponies stand and take everything with us when we leave. Yes, including that.

How we look after kids and ponies

The riders

Every ride is led on foot by one of us, with a hand on the lead the whole time. Kids wear a fitted helmet, and we help each one on and off.

We match riders to ponies by size, so a nine-year-old won't end up on Peaches. If a child is nervous, they can brush and pet first. Nobody gets put on a pony who doesn't want to be.

The ponies

They live on pasture at our place and see the vet and farrier on a regular schedule. At parties they get water, shade, and breaks between rides.

If it's too hot or a pony seems tired, we stop. And if a storm is coming, we'll move your party to another date at no charge.

From parents

Questions people ask

Don't see yours? Text us at (555) 766-9725.

How much space do we need?

Less than you'd think. A patch of grass about 30 by 40 feet is plenty for a riding loop, and a driveway or quiet cul-de-sac works too. If you're using a public park, check whether they need a permit for animals.

Are there age or weight limits?

Our ponies are best for kids about 2 to 10. Weight limits run from 45 lb on Peaches to 90 lb on Starlight. We'll match each rider to a pony that fits.

What happens if it rains?

A light drizzle is fine. For storms, lightning, or serious heat, we'll move you to another date for free. Some families just move the loop into a garage or carport.

Where does the trailer park?

We tow a two-horse trailer behind a pickup and need roughly two and a half car lengths of street or driveway. We get there 15 to 20 minutes early so riding starts on time.

How does payment work?

Nothing is due when you send the form. Once we've confirmed your date, a $50 deposit holds it. The rest is due on the day by cash, check, card, Venmo, or Zelle.
