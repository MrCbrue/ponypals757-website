Safety and care

Riders and guests

Rides are guided by a handler, and we match every rider's age and comfort level to the right session. Pony Play Dates welcome ages 1+, while Pony Rides and Lessons are for ages 2+ with a 150 lb riding weight limit.

First-time or nervous riders can go at their own pace, starting with grooming and decorating before stepping into the ring.

Our horses and ponies

For photo rentals, we handle and pose your preferred horse and groom them to match your vision. For Pony Parties, we deck out your Pony Pals to fit your theme.

Weather and travel plans are always coordinated with the horses' safety and comfort first.

Questions people ask

Include any questions in your booking request and we'll go over them with you before anything is finalized.

Where are you located, and do you travel?

We are located in Hampton, Virginia. Ring rides, Pony Play Dates, and lessons take place here, and we can take customers from anywhere with owner discretion.

What are the age and weight limits?

Pony Play Dates are for ages 1+. Pony Rides and Lessons are for ages 2+ with a 150 lb weight limit.

What is included in a Pony Party?

Pony Parties start at $275 and include celebrating your event with your Pony Pals decked out to fit your theme and taking your guests for a ride. Final pricing depends on your event details and travel.

How do Pony Rental for Photos sessions work?

Photo rentals are $275 for 1 hour with your preferred horse, including handling and posing of the horse groomed to suit your vision.

Do I pay when submitting the request form?

No. Sending the form simply holds your inquiry until we follow up with you to confirm availability and final details.
