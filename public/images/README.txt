Photos for the website
======================

Save your own photos in this folder using the file names below. They will show up
on the site automatically. Until a photo exists, the site shows a solid color panel
in the pony's coat color.

  hero.jpg        Main photo at the top of the homepage (portrait, roughly 5:6)
  buttercup.jpg   Buttercup (landscape, 4:3)
  starlight.jpg   Starlight (landscape, 4:3)
  barnaby.jpg     Barnaby   (landscape, 4:3)
  peaches.jpg     Peaches   (landscape, 4:3)

Tips: use real photos taken at parties (with the parents' permission if kids are
visible), daylight, ponies at eye level. Around 1600px wide is plenty.
